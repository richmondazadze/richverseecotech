WEBSITE PROJECT BRIEF — Richverse Ecotech
Copy this into a document or email. One or two sentences per prompt are enough.

1. Current site and owner:
   Public URL (if any); who edits it today?
2. Customer and task:
   Who comes to the site? What do they need to finish?
3. Where the task breaks:
   How do they start? Where do they stop? Describe one example without personal data.
4. Keep, change, or investigate:
   Existing URLs, content, booking/payment tools, languages, integrations or operating constraints.
5. What success looks like:
   Starting from __________, the visitor can __________. If __________ fails, the site should __________.
6. Ownership after handover:
   Who will hold the domain, hosting and publishing accounts, and who will check delivery?
7. Commercial constraints:
   Desired launch window, reason for the date, budget range if known, who can approve trade-offs.
8. Ask each studio for:
   Included work, exclusions, dependencies, one-time and recurring costs, acceptance checks and support.

Example from our own website:
   Starting on a service page, the visitor can open the enquiry form with that service selected.
   On a delivery failure, the visitor keeps the draft and can retry or use direct email.
